How standard TF-IDF Cosine Similarity Works

The MovieRecommender engine computes TF-IDF weight vectors across Genres, Plot Summaries, Mood tags, Directors, and Casts. Cosine similarity measures the angle between document vectors in high-dimensional feature space:

$$\text{Similarity}(A, B) = \frac{A \cdot B}{\|A\| \|B\|} = \frac{\sum A_i B_i}{\sqrt{\sum A_i^2} \sqrt{\sum B_i^2}}$$
